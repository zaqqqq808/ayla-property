## How I actually scored these

Every gym review you read in Bali is either a paid post or somebody's mate doing them a favour. So before I tear into anyone, here is exactly how the numbers were made.

Twelve categories, each scored out of 5. Then weighted, because not everything matters equally.

**Weighted at 1.4x, the stuff that actually matters:**

- Equipment
- Gym space, meaning layout, size and changing rooms
- Hygiene
- Recovery

**Weighted at 1.2x, the stuff we all pretend does not matter:**

- Location
- Restaurant
- Vibes
- Extras, meaning co working, run clubs and events

**Weighted at 1x:**

- Building quality and style
- Combat
- Community
- Pool

That gives a maximum of 72 points, which I have converted to a score out of 10 so it reads like a normal review. Because a couple of gyms land within a rounding error of each other, I have put the raw points in the table too.

Five things worth saying before the list.

**The pool is not recovery, so I stopped scoring them together.** Recovery is the ice bath, the sauna, the steam room, the cold plunge. The stuff that is actually doing something to your body after you train. A pool is a pool. It is lovely, it is why half of Canggu picks a gym, and it belongs in the scorecard, but it belongs at 1x alongside the other nice to haves rather than sitting at 1.4x pretending to be a recovery protocol. Splitting them cost three gyms a point of recovery they were only holding because they had a nice pool.

**Vibes and community are not the same thing either.** Vibes is how a place feels while you are in it. The smell, the sound, the light, the aesthetic. Community is whether you belong. Whether anyone learns your name, whether it is easy to make friends, whether you would still turn up if you had nobody to meet. A gym can nail one and completely miss the other, and two of the gyms below do exactly that in opposite directions.

**Nothing gets counted twice.** That is the rule that runs through this whole scorecard. A martial arts academy is a combat facility, so it scores under combat and does not also inflate the extras column. A busy, sociable room scores under community and does not also inflate vibes. A nice pool scores under pool and does not also inflate recovery. Applying that rule honestly has moved five gyms, and not in the direction their owners would like.

**Changing rooms sit inside gym space, at the heaviest weighting.** You spend real time in there, usually at your least comfortable, and it is the single most reliable tell of whether a gym actually respects the people paying it.

**Combat is only weighted at 1x and it still swings the mid table harder than anything else.** It has the widest spread of anything here, a perfect 5 down to an actual zero.

## 8. Undefeated, 6.4/10

Undefeated sits in Canggu, and the location is the least convenient on this list along with Nirvana. That is the worst thing I am going to say about it, so bear with me.

The building is a good one and the look is the whole personality of the place. Anime themed artwork throughout, done properly rather than done as a gimmick. It is a cool, quirky gym and it has an identity, which is rarer around here than it should be. That is why the vibes score holds at a 4 while gyms twice its size score lower.

The machines are great. Exactly what you need to train hard and nothing you do not. Along with Reload and Obsidian, this is the better equipment tier. The space is the smallest here, which is the honest reason it scores a 3 rather than anything to do with how it is laid out. Hygiene is solid. And then the recovery, which is the bit that surprised me. I heard "indoor recovery" and thought, what? In Bali? Then I saw it and stopped cringing immediately, because it works. The pool is a 3, which is to say it exists and it is fine.

Here is the key thing about Undefeated. It is never rammed. On a Tuesday at 5pm that is worth more than a smoothie bar. The flip side is a community score of 2, the lowest here. A quiet room is a lovely place to train and a hard place to make friends. You will get your session done and you will probably leave without speaking to anybody.

On the food and the rest of it, do not come here expecting either. The restaurant is fine and there is no co working space worth the name. It is not trying to do that, and I respect it for not pretending.

It finishes last because the scoring rewards breadth and Undefeated deliberately has none. That is a scoring artefact more than a criticism. If you want a focused room, good iron and no queue, this is arguably the best value membership on the list.

**Score:** Equipment 4, Space 3, Hygiene 4, Recovery 4, Location 3, Restaurant 3, Vibes 4, Extras 2, Building 4, Combat 2, Community 2, Pool 3.

## 7. Obsidian II, 7.2/10

The location is good and easy, no complaints there.

The building is where it starts to go wrong. It has no taste. There is no design idea holding the place together, and the bits that have been added feel bolted on rather than planned. Chief among them, they added a water slide. A water slide. Who is using that? Which member walked in, saw a water slide, and thought yes, this is the gym for me. Vibes scores a 2, the lowest in that category on this list, and the building a 3.

Now the good part, and it is genuinely good. The equipment is excellent, one of the best sets of iron in Bali. The space is big and if you are there to move heavy things and leave, Obsidian II serves you better than most of the pretty ones above it. Then the changing rooms, which are poor. And the recovery, which is a 3 and the joint weakest here. It is uninviting, which is a strange thing to build and then not make people want to use. Splitting the pool out into its own category is what exposed that, because the water slide was never recovery and now it is not scored as though it were. The pool itself is a 3.

The key point is that this is a hardcore gym goer's gym and it knows it. The community is a genuine 4, which surprises people. Serious rooms tend to attract serious regulars, and regulars recognise each other.

The restaurant is decent and the extras are thin. It just comes off a bit tacky overall. I want to be fair here though, so I am going to visit again before I lock this score in.

**Score:** Equipment 5, Space 4, Hygiene 4, Recovery 3, Location 4, Restaurant 4, Vibes 2, Extras 3, Building 3, Combat 4, Community 4, Pool 3.

## 6. Nirvana, 7.2/10

The location is awful. It sits right in the tourist part, which is exactly why it is permanently busy, but getting there is a chore and getting out of the car park is worse.

The building is nothing special either. There is no architectural idea here, no material anybody agonised over. It is a room. Both of those scores are a 3 and neither of them is what people come for.

The equipment is the weakest on this list. Enough to train, not enough to progress on if you are serious about lifting. The space is decent and hygiene is fine. The recovery, though, is genuinely great and one of only two 5s in that category on this list. It survived the split from the pool intact, which is more than three other gyms managed. The pool itself is an ordinary 3.

And now the actual point of Nirvana. It has the best community of any gym in Canggu, the only 5 in that category on the entire list. If you have just landed, know nobody, and want a place where it is genuinely easy to make friends, this is the answer and the total score is irrelevant to you. But be clear about what it is. This is a social club that happens to own dumbbells. It is a place for classes and for fannying around, not a weight training gym. Its vibes score dropped to a 3 once community was pulled out into its own category, and that is the honest reading. What people love about Nirvana is the people. The room itself is unremarkable.

The restaurant is good and there is a decent programme of things going on around the gym, which feeds straight back into why the place is so sociable.

Sixth, and the correct choice for a lot of people reading this.

**Score:** Equipment 3, Space 4, Hygiene 4, Recovery 5, Location 3, Restaurant 4, Vibes 3, Extras 4, Building 3, Combat 2, Community 5, Pool 3.

## 5. Surya Gym, 7.5/10

Decent location, and a properly built building. Solid, well made, no complaints about the fabric of the place at all, which is why building scores a 5.

What it is not is designed. Nobody has thought about the light or the sound or what walking in should feel like. Vibes is a 3, and for a gym this focused on the actual training that is a fair trade rather than a failure.

The equipment is a little old. Perfectly good, but a little old. Nothing about the machines is going to make you post a story. The space works and the layout is sensible. Then the changing rooms, which are grim. Dingy, and not clean enough for a gym charging what it charges in a climate like this. The rooftop recovery is a great touch and the sort of thing you only appreciate after you have used it a few times, though scored on its own merits rather than lumped in with the pool it settles at a 4 rather than a 5. The pool is a 3.

Here is the key point, and it is a big one. Surya has the best fight facilities in Bali and it is not close. Not "there is a bag in the corner" level. Proper equipment, proper space, an actual [fight club programme](https://www.suryagymbali.com/fight-club/). If you want to box or do muay thai seriously in Canggu, this is the conversation and everywhere else is a footnote. It is the only 5 for combat on this list and it deserves it.

On food and extras, this is where it thins out fast. The restaurant is fine. Outside the fight programme there are pool tables on the ground floor and not a lot more. No co working worth the name, no run club, nothing built around the gym rather than inside it. The academy itself is already scored under combat, where it belongs, so it does not get to count twice and pad out the extras column. Scored honestly, extras is a 2, and that is what puts a gym this good at one thing down in fifth.

**Score:** Equipment 4, Space 4, Hygiene 4, Recovery 4, Location 4, Restaurant 4, Vibes 3, Extras 2, Building 5, Combat 5, Community 3, Pool 3.

## 4. Rite Gym, 7.9/10

This was my personal favourite for years, so understand that everything below is said with affection.

Best location on the list. Very close to the beach in [Pererenan](/invest/pererenan/), which matters more than people admit when you are deciding whether to actually go at 6am. And architecturally it is one of the best gyms I have been in anywhere. Industrial brutalist done properly rather than done as a mood board, with the concrete and the proportions actually thought through.

The equipment is good without being remarkable, and the space is one of the best laid out here. Then the changing rooms, which are too dark and too dirty. Dark colours are a bad choice for a bathroom. You need to see dirt easily, otherwise germs get missed and the hygiene etiquette goes with it. There are also no spray wipes on the gym floor, which in a hot country is not a small thing, and that is what drags hygiene down to a 3. The recovery is the only one on this list that is truly trying to be zen. Chill music, a secluded ice bath, and essential oils in the steam room, which should be mandatory by law in 2026. It scores a 4 rather than a 5 because the facilities themselves are smaller than what Omni and Nirvana offer, not because the thinking is worse. The pool is an ordinary 3 and nobody comes here for it.

The key thing about Rite is that it is the only gym in Canggu where somebody has thought about what the place smells like. It gained the most from separating vibes and community and it deserved to, because on pure sensory terms it is the strongest room here. Rite have little details right. It is truly designed by gym goers. The boxing room is the best I have seen anywhere. And then they will run a yoga class in it, which defeats the entire point. They do the same thing upstairs, running classes in the space normal people are trying to train in.

Then the food, which is terrible. Very basic, and in the evening you get eaten alive by mosquitos while you eat it. It is the lowest restaurant score on this list by a distance.

Renovations are underway. I will re review at Christmas and I expect this score to move.

**Score:** Equipment 4, Space 5, Hygiene 3, Recovery 4, Location 5, Restaurant 2, Vibes 5, Extras 4, Building 5, Combat 4, Community 3, Pool 3.

## 3. Reload, 8.1/10

Great location, easy to get to, and then a building that nobody designed with much care. The look is the weakest part of the whole operation. Glaring blue lights in the evening, bad artwork, and a layout that feels assembled rather than planned.

That is also where separating vibes and community really bites. Strip out the crowd and what is actually left is those blue lights and that artwork. Vibes is a 3, and it was only ever a 4 because the room is full.

Now the good part. The machines are the best of any gym in Bali, full stop. If you care about the quality of the iron more than anything else, you go here and you stop reading. The roof space is good and the room is big. The showers are shoved out the back near the bins, which is a bit grim. And the recovery area already looks like it is coming apart, which is remarkable given the place is not old. That is a 3 and it is the joint weakest recovery on this list. The pool, on the other hand, is a proper 4 and a big part of why people actually spend their afternoons here.

The key thing about Reload is that it is by far the busiest gym in Bali and I have never fully worked out why. All those people do add up to something real though, which is why community scores a 4.

The restaurant is fantastic, genuinely one of the better places to eat in the area regardless of whether you trained, and it is the only 5 for food on this list. Honestly, Reload is a beach club you can train at. Take it on those terms and it is excellent. It finishes third by 0.4 of a point and it earns every one of them at the bar rather than in the gym. They are expanding, so who knows what turns up next.

**Score:** Equipment 5, Space 4, Hygiene 5, Recovery 3, Location 5, Restaurant 5, Vibes 3, Extras 3, Building 4, Combat 3, Community 4, Pool 4.

## 2. House of Kairos, 8.1/10

Great location, just down the road from Open House. And then the most beautiful gym in Bali. It is not close.

It resembles a Soho House more than a gym. Art deco through the restaurant and the co working space, genuinely stylish, with a level of finish nothing else here comes near. You feel good working out here. You feel like you are part of a luxury experience rather than a gym membership, and that is worth something.

The equipment is Technogym, so it is not the best iron on this list, but it is brand new Technogym and it is more than enough for 95% of people. The space is good and hygiene is a 5. The recovery is very good without being the best here, a 4, with cinnamon essential oil in the steam room, which is exactly the sort of decision that tells you somebody with taste is in charge. Then the small stuff lets it down slightly. There is no shower gel next to the shower. In a place this considered, that omission is loud.

And then the pool, which is the only 5 on this entire list. Best pool by a distance, and I mean that literally, it is the only pool here I actually want to swim in rather than photograph. Best loungers too. Separating the pool out of recovery cost most gyms a point and it is the single reason Kairos holds second place rather than third.

The key point, and it is the big one. No combat. Zero. Sometimes you just want to hit the bags for a while, burn some extra calories, hone a bit of technique. You cannot. Second place while scoring an actual zero in a category tells you exactly how strong the rest of the card is. The community is also only a 3. It is a beautiful room full of people having a nice time separately, which is not the same as belonging somewhere.

The restaurant is good and the co working space is stylish and usable. The whole package is about as pleasant as a gym gets. It is 0.4 points ahead of Reload, which is close enough that you should pick between them on what you actually want rather than on the order they appear in here.

**Score:** Equipment 4, Space 4, Hygiene 5, Recovery 4, Location 5, Restaurant 4, Vibes 5, Extras 4, Building 5, Combat 0, Community 3, Pool 5.

## 1. Omni, 8.9/10

The location is good rather than perfect, and the building is excellent. Stylish without being smug, which is a harder balance to strike around here than it sounds. The vibes in the evening are the other thing. Some gyms empty out and die at 6pm. Omni gets better, and that is the lighting and the music as much as the people.

The equipment is a 4, so it is not the best iron in Bali and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Everything else in the heavy weighted block is perfect. Space 5, hygiene 5, recovery 5. Nothing else on this list manages that, and those are three of the four categories that count for the most. The infrared sauna is a genuine differentiator rather than a marketing line, and there are two outdoor training areas, which in this climate is the correct decision that almost nobody else makes. The pool is a solid 4, second only to Kairos.

Omni is also the only gym near the top of this list whose recovery score survived the pool being pulled out of it. That is the whole argument for splitting them. Its 5 was always about the sauna and the facilities, not about having somewhere nice to lie down.

The key point about Omni is simply that it never gives you a reason to complain, which sounds like faint praise until you try to find another gym here you can say it about. Every other gym on this list has a hole in it somewhere. Reload has the recovery and the showers. Kairos has no combat. Surya has the changing rooms and nothing outside the fighting. Rite has the food and the hygiene. Omni has a combat score of 3 and that is the whole list of complaints.

And the co working area is the best in Canggu. I mean that flatly. Not the best gym co working area, the best co working area. If you train in the morning and then work until two, the maths on an Omni membership starts looking silly in your favour. The restaurant is good, the community is a 4, and the extras column is the strongest here.

It wins by 5.8 points, the largest gap anywhere on this table, because it is the only gym on this list that is bad at nothing.

**Score:** Equipment 4, Space 5, Hygiene 5, Recovery 5, Location 4, Restaurant 4, Vibes 5, Extras 5, Building 5, Combat 3, Community 4, Pool 4.

## So which one should you actually join

- **You want the fewest compromises:** Omni. It is first for a reason, and by the biggest margin on the table.
- **You have just arrived and know nobody:** Nirvana. Sixth on this list and the correct answer for you by a mile.
- **You fight, or you want to:** Surya Gym. Not a debate. Bring your own flip flops.
- **You train in the morning and work all afternoon:** Omni. The co working alone justifies it.
- **You actually swim:** House of Kairos, then Omni or Reload. Nothing else here has a pool worth planning your day around.
- **You want to feel like your life is going well:** House of Kairos.
- **You only care about the machines:** Reload, then Obsidian II, then Undefeated.
- **You live in Pererenan:** Rite Gym, and the renovation may well move it up this list by Christmas.
- **You want to train hard, cheap, with no queue:** Undefeated.

Which is the real lesson of this list. The winner is the gym with no weaknesses, but the right gym for you is almost always the one that is brilliant at the single thing you actually care about. Nirvana is sixth and it is the best answer for a lot of people reading this. Read the category scores, not the total.

And if you can afford two memberships, the honest answer that nobody wants to hear is Surya plus House of Kairos. One for training, one for living.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best gym in Canggu?**

On a weighted score across twelve categories, Omni at 8.9 out of 10. It is the only gym in Canggu with no weak category, and it scores a perfect 5 on space, hygiene and recovery, three of the four heaviest weighted categories. House of Kairos is second at 8.1, just ahead of Reload.

**Which Canggu gym has the best pool?**

House of Kairos, and it is the only 5 for the pool on this list. It is the one pool here worth planning an afternoon around, and it comes with the best loungers too. Omni and Reload both score a 4. Everywhere else is a 3.

**Which gym in Canggu has the best recovery?**

Omni and Nirvana, both on a 5. Rite Gym in Pererenan scores a 4 but has the most considered recovery experience of any of them, with a secluded ice bath, calm music and essential oils in the steam room. Note that the pool is scored separately here, so these are recovery facilities in the strict sense.

**Which Canggu gym has the best community?**

Nirvana, and it is the only 5 for community on this list. It finishes sixth overall because it is a weak weight training gym, but if you are new to Bali and want somewhere it is easy to make friends, it is the best answer here. Omni, Reload and Obsidian II all score 4.

**Which Bali gym has the best equipment?**

Reload, comfortably. The machines there are the best on the island. Obsidian II and Undefeated are the next tier.

**Which is the best gym in Bali for boxing and muay thai?**

Surya Gym, and it is not close. It is the only gym here scoring a perfect 5 for combat, with a proper fight club programme rather than a bag in the corner. It finishes fifth overall only because it offers very little outside the fighting. Rite Gym has the best single boxing room, though it also gets used for yoga classes.

**Which Canggu gym has the best changing rooms?**

None of them are good, which is the running theme of this review. Omni and House of Kairos are the cleanest. Surya Gym and Rite Gym are the two worst offenders and both lose real points for it.

**Which Canggu gym has the best food?**

Reload, and it is the only 5 for the restaurant on this list. It is genuinely one of the better places to eat in the area whether or not you trained. Rite Gym is the worst at a 2.

**Which Canggu gym is best for digital nomads?**

Omni, for the co working space. House of Kairos is a close second if you care more about the environment than the desk setup.

**How much do gyms cost in Canggu?**

Membership pricing changes constantly and most of these gyms run promotions, so check directly with each one rather than trusting a number in a blog post. Expect the premium end of this list to sit meaningfully above the local average.
